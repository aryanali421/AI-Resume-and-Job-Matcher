# Redline – Resume × Job Post Gap Report

**Know what matches, what's missing, and what may be costing you the interview.**

Redline compares a resume with a job posting and produces a marked-up gap report. It is intended to help applicants understand how their experience aligns with a role and identify areas where their resume could communicate that fit more clearly.

## Core workflow

1. **Add your resume** – Paste resume text or upload a supported PDF or TXT file.
2. **Add the job post** – Paste the job description for the role you're targeting.
3. **Review the gap report** – Examine the matches, missing requirements, and resume lines that may weaken the application.
4. **Improve your resume** – Use the findings to make accurate, evidence-based edits.

## What the analysis should help identify

- Relevant skills and experience that already match the job post.
- Important requirements that are missing or not clearly demonstrated.
- Resume statements that are vague, weak, or poorly aligned with the role.
- Opportunities to make existing achievements clearer and more specific.

## Important notes

- Redline should not invent qualifications, experience, skills, or achievements.
- A missing keyword does not necessarily mean the applicant lacks the underlying skill; it may simply be absent from the resume wording.
- Review every suggested change and keep the resume truthful.
- The exact upload limits and supported formats should be confirmed against the deployed application.
